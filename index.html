Quadruped Robot Dog

A twelve-servo walking robot running on an ESP32, with a gait controller and inverse kinematics solved on-device.

The obvious servo library blocked for a full 20 ms PWM frame per write — twelve sequential writes cost 240 ms and the gait visibly stuttered. I dropped to the ESP32's LEDC peripheral and drove the channels directly, which took the update loop under 2 ms.
